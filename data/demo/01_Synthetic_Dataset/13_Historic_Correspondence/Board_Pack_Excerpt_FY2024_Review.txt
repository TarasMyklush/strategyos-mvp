MIZAN GROUP — BOARD PACK EXCERPT (STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL)
Tamween Pharma Distribution — FY2024 performance review
Prepared by: BU Finance, February 2025 | Scope: BU total, with Central Region division detail

1. HEADLINES
- BU revenue SAR 2,120M (+9.3% vs 2023 standalone 1,940M). First full year under Mizan ownership.
- Central Region division (pilot ledger): net revenue SAR 633M (+7.3%). EBITDA margin 6.4% BU-wide.
- NUPCO regional framework won October 2024 (Central Region lots 3/5/7) — the strategic win of the year; ~SAR 50M+ annualised from 2025.

2. REVENUE DRIVERS (Central Region division)
- Modern Pharmacy +SAR 20M: chain expansion, notably Red Sea Global Healthcare growth.
- Government +SAR 18M: NUPCO call-offs began November.
- Workforce Health flat: KAUST Health Centre volumes up, but the 12% retro rebate agreed at 2024 renewal offsets nearly all growth. Contribution review recommended.

3. COST & INTEGRATION
- ERP migration completed July 2024; PO module live H2 (PO coverage 61% of H2 spend).
- Logistics: transitioned to Gulf Cold-Chain Logistics (CT-2023-014) from February at SAR 176K/month. Bahr Freight customs engagement ended December — spend ran ~SAR 1.0M incl. disputed double-billed months (Mar/Apr/May); no credit obtained.
- Utilities +19% YoY, driven by Qassim WH (+35% vs comparable sites) — co-location recharge to Pharmacy Retail still not established.
- Integration one-offs SAR 14M (ERP, rebranding, retention).

4. WATCH ITEMS FOR 2025
- Vendor master hygiene: 6 supplier families trade via multiple registered entities (incl. Al-Rashid Pharma x2 with same Tax ID).
- Mediterranean Pharma credit note SAR 128K (Nov) — ensure offset on next cycle.
- Payables controls baseline (Internal Audit) deferred to 2025 due to migration workload.
