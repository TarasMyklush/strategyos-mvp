SUBJECT: Acquisition close — 100-day integration plan (Finance workstream)

Team,

The acquisition of Tamween Pharma Distribution closed on 8 January 2024. Welcome to Mizan Group.

Finance workstream priorities for the first 100 days:

1. ERP migration. Tamween's legacy TTS system will be migrated onto the Mizan group ERP template. Target go-live July 2024. Note: the group template's account descriptions date from Mizan's FMCG-distribution era (e.g. "Revenue - Catering", "COGS - Food"). To avoid remapping three years of comparatives, we will retain the legacy descriptions and publish a mapping guide. Descriptions are cosmetic; postings are pharma.

2. Purchase order discipline. Tamween currently operates without a formal PO system. The group PO module goes live with the ERP migration (July 2024). Until then, spend is approved by email — Finance must retain approval trails.

3. Cash pooling. From February 2024, Tamween's excess operating cash sweeps monthly to Mizan Group treasury (previously swept to Tamween Holdco). Booked to account 2900.

4. Controls review. Internal Audit will baseline Tamween's payables controls in H2 2024. Early observations suggest gaps in duplicate-payment prevention and vendor master hygiene (several suppliers appear to trade under multiple registered entities).

The Central Region (Riyadh) division — 4 of the 12 warehouses, roughly 30% of BU revenue — will serve as the pilot ledger for the integration before national rollout.

Sara
