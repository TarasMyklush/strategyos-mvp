AD-HOC BRIEFING — API supply concentration risk
Date: 9 April 2026
Compiler: Mizan Group Research — Manufacturing team
Distribution: Manufacturing CEO, Group COO, Procurement CoE

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CONTEXT
74% of Mizan Pharma Manufacturing's top-50 APIs are sourced from
India or China. Recent INR weakness and CNY tightening allocation,
plus G7 trade-flow noise around generic pharma supply, makes the
concentration topic urgent.

WHAT'S HAPPENING
  • India regulatory tightening: WHO-GMP renewals required at 3-yr
    cycle; 8% of Indian generic exporters delayed in Q1 2026.
  • China: Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient export licensing slowed
    over 2025 — some 12% of categories experiencing 30-60 day delays.
  • Generic API blend price +6-9% YoY across our basket.
  • Semaglutide API specifically: +18% YoY on global GLP-1 supply
    tightness; Korean and Chinese capacity ramping.

OPERATIONAL POSTURE
  • 90-day strategic safety stock policy: in place for top-30 APIs;
    extending to top-50 by Q4-2026.
  • Dual-sourcing programme: 38% of top-50 APIs dual-sourced today
    (target 100% by 2028). Q2 2026 dual-source qualification programme
    on schedule.
  • Lifera partnership for biosimilars: signed March 2026; first GLP-1
    biosimilar end-2027.

WATCHPOINTS
  • Continued INR weakness: directionally a tailwind to Mizan but only
    if Indian regulators don't tighten on lower-margin exports.
  • CNY policy: tightening continues; may extend to mAb cell-culture
    media and biotech-grade inputs in 2027.
  • G7 trade-flow signals: limited direct impact on KSA imports today;
    secondary impact through global pricing.
