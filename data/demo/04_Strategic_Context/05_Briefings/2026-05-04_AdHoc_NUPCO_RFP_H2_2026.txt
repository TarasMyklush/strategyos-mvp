Date: 4 May 2026

WHAT HAPPENED
NUPCO H2-2026 master supply RFP cycle opens 1 August. Mizan is
bidding 6 categories. This is the single largest commercial event
for the year for Tamween Pharma Distribution + Mizan Pharma Logistics.

CATEGORIES MIZAN IS BIDDING
  1. Sterile injectables (annual value SAR ~220M; Manufacturing-led)
  2. Oncology (cytotoxics + supportive care, SAR ~180M)
  3. Biologics + GLP-1 supply (SAR ~140M)
  4. Cold-chain distribution (SAR ~480M — the largest, Logistics-led)
  5. Retail pharmacy supply (SAR ~290M)
  6. Vaccines + immunisation (SAR ~95M)

  Total addressable: ~SAR 1.40B annual; Mizan target capture SAR 580-650M
  (~14% of NUPCO public-pharma spend).

COMPETITIVE READ
  • Cold-chain distribution: Mizan vs Mohebi (in JV with Tamer) is the
    primary contest. Dammam hub commissioning Q2-2026 is the differentiator.
  • Sterile injectables: SPIMACO + Tabuk + Jamjoom + Hikma are the
    competitors. Mizan competitive on Saudi-Made portfolio share.
  • Biologics + GLP-1: limited local capacity; Mizan partnership-led
    bid should clear.
  • Retail pharmacy supply: Al-Nahdi captive supply + Whites Online +
    Mizan Pharmacy Retail are the credible bidders.

BID STRATEGY
  • Saudi-Made share emphasis: Mizan portfolio 32% vs market avg ~22%.
  • Cold-chain integration: only Mizan can bid the end-to-end stack.
  • Price discipline: NUPCO weights Saudi-Made + GDP-WHO at ~40% of
    selection; price ~35%; capacity + reliability ~25%.

CAPACITY READINESS
  • Sudair Line 8 commissioning Q4-2026 (sterile injectables capacity).
  • Dammam cold-chain hub Q2-2026 (commissioning).
  • API safety stocks: extended to top-50 by Q4-2026 (in flight).
  • Saudisation pipeline: pre-emptive hiring continues.

TIMELINE
  • RFP cycle opens: 1 August 2026
  • Site visits: 1-15 September
  • Best & final: 30 September
  • Decision: 31 October 2026
  • Contract effective: 1 January 2027

CAPITAL IMPLICATIONS
  • Bid bond requirement: SAR 35-40M aggregate
  • Mobilisation advance: not material for distribution; SAR 25M for cold-chain
  • Working capital: SAR 60-85M peak in months 3-9 of execution
