AD-HOC BRIEFING — Cardiology consultant gap, Mizan Healthcare Services
Date: 19 May 2026
Compiler: Mizan Group Research — Healthcare team
Distribution: Healthcare Services CEO, Group CFO, Group CHRO

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT IS HAPPENING
Two cardiology consultants at Mizan Healthcare Services Riyadh have
given notice and depart 31 May 2026. Both accepted positions at
Dr Sulaiman Al-Habib (one Riyadh, one planned NEOM centre).

WHY IT MATTERS
Cardiology is Mizan Healthcare Services' highest-margin specialty.
The 2 departing consultants generated ~40 elective cases/week of
throughput at average SAR 24k/case — ~SAR 4M revenue exposure for
the June-July window.

REPLACEMENT PIPELINE
  • Two replacement consultants signed offers in April. Onboarding
    15 June 2026 (one Saudi national, one expatriate).
  • SCFHS accreditation: complete for Saudi-national, in-progress
    for expatriate (expected 2 weeks).
  • Insurance-panel listing: in flight for both with Bupa Arabia,
    Tawuniya, Walaa.

BROADER SECTOR CONTEXT
  • Cardiology consultant supply nationally is tight.
    Estimated 240 consultants serving private + public; demand ~290.
  • Habib expanding aggressively at the expense of mid-scale operators.
  • Recruitment cycle: 9-12 months from offer to billing.

OPERATIONAL IMPLICATIONS
  • Bed occupancy drop: 71% (vs 78% Q1-2026) for the 4-6 week gap.
  • Outpatient referral pipeline: 1,240 referrals for June, intact.
  • Revenue impact: ~SAR 4M shortfall to Q2 plan; recovers in Q3.

MITIGATION
  • Locum cover: SAR 280k for 4 weeks via UK and UAE network.
  • Marketing pause: incremental outpatient cardiology marketing
    paused until 1 July.
  • Insurance reassurance: Bupa Arabia notified.
