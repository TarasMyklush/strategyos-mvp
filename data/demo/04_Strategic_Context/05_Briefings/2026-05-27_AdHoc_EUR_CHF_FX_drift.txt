ONE-LINE
Both EUR and CHF are structurally higher than at any point since 2014.
Mizan's European + Swiss pharma import lines (Sanofi, Servier, Roche,
Novartis) are leaking margin where hedge programmes are not actively
applied. Tamween Pharma Distribution's Q1-2026 leakage event is the
case study.
