Shared mental model

The governed decision loop

StrategyOS is neither a reporting dashboard nor an autonomous decision maker. It is the controlled path between evidence and accountable action.

1Connect

Bring approved plans and permitted company sources into a governed run.

2Verify

Validate quality, lineage, identity, period and reconciliation before use.

3Explain

Calculate the position, identify material movement and attach evidence.

4Decide

Route the issue to the role with the authority and context to make the call.

5Record

Preserve review, approval, execution state and the resulting audit trail.

What stays constant

Trust is structural

The interface can adapt to a CEO, CFO, reviewer or operator. These rules remain invariant.

Evidence before assertion

Claims link to governed source material and calculation context. Missing evidence remains visible.

Authority before access

Role, domain and action rights are resolved server-side before retrieval or execution.

Deterministic numbers

KPIs, plan comparisons and reconciliations are calculated by governed code, not invented by a language model.

Human approval for consequence

Recommendations may be prepared automatically; consequential action remains review-gated and auditable.
