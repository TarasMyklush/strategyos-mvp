Product guide

How StrategyOS works

StrategyOS turns approved plans and source-backed company records into a decision-ready CEO briefing. It shows the headline, the reason it moved, the evidence behind it, and the action that belongs at executive level. Source traceability is separate from independent review.

One governed decision surface

No invented values, no hidden case work, and no “running” agent unless execution has actually been recorded.

The operating loop

From source to decision

The interface stays compact; supporting detail is revealed only when it helps the decision.

  1. 1
    Read the position

    The opening statement identifies the most material KPI or governance issue for the current reporting period.

  2. 2
    Inspect what moved it

    Select a KPI to compare actuals with plan, review its trend, see the contributing business lines, and open the calculation trail.

  3. 3
    Decide at the right altitude

    StrategyOS separates CEO intervention from work that should remain with the accountable executive, reviewer, or specialist agent.

The product

What each area is for

  • Diagnostics

    The CEO briefing: headline KPIs, movements, concerns, developments, calendar context, modelling, and leadership material.

  • AI Assistants

    Role-aligned interfaces for accountable leaders. They show responsibilities, current priorities, decision needs, and escalation paths.

  • Agents

    Specialist work such as analysis and audit. An agent appears only when the application has a recorded task or audit trail to show.

  • KPI evidence

    The source and relationship view behind headline figures, including missing-input and evidence boundaries.

  • Hermes

    The decision dialogue. Hermes answers from the current governed packet, explains the implication, and distinguishes evidence from uncertainty.

Shared visual language

How to read the colours

Direction is KPI-aware. “More” is not automatically good.

Favourable

Green means the KPI moved in the beneficial direction: revenue, margin or cash above plan—or operating cost below plan.

Unfavourable

Red means the KPI moved against the plan: revenue, margin or cash below plan—or operating cost above plan.

Watch or governance

Amber is reserved for review gates, incomplete evidence, or a watch state. Neutral styling means no safe comparison is available.

Trust boundary

What StrategyOS will not pretend

  • No estimate presented as an actual

    If an input or aligned comparator is missing, the conclusion is withheld and the missing information is named.

  • No execution without a record

    Idle, disabled, planned, or catalogue-only agents are not presented as running work.

  • No case-level noise at CEO altitude

    Operational work remains delegated unless its aggregate exposure or decision threshold requires executive intervention.
